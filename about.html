About Us

A small Kansas company that does a few things well

Prairie Summit Technologies doesn't have a tidy one-line pitch, and honestly, we've made peace with that. Here's the fuller picture.

Our Story

Built on doing more than one thing

Prairie Summit Technologies is based in Wichita, Kansas, and was built around a simple idea: a small team can be genuinely good at more than one thing at once, if it stays disciplined about each one.

Instead of picking a single lane, we operate across three: 3D printing and fabrication, general commerce, and technology & tooling for crypto-adjacent and technical workflows. Each line stands on its own, but they share the same people, the same standards, and the same willingness to figure things out from first principles.

We're not a big company pretending to be small, or a small company pretending to be big. We're a Kansas operation that takes on real work across a few different areas, and tries to do each of them properly.

Grounded

Wichita is home base, not just a mailing address. We build things that hold up.

Broad by design

Three business lines aren't a distraction from focus — they're how we stay resilient and interesting.

Straightforward
