Services

Three disciplines. One shop.

Here's what we actually do, broken out plainly — no jargon dressing-up. If your project spans more than one of these, that's normal for us.

01

3D Printing

From a rough idea to a printed part

We design, model, and print — handling one-off prototypes as easily as small production batches. That includes functional prototypes, replacement parts, enclosures, fixtures, and custom one-off pieces for personal or business use.

  • 3D modeling & design-for-print consultation
  • FDM printing in a range of engineering & consumer filaments
  • Prototyping, iteration, and functional testing
  • Small-batch production runs
  • Post-processing: sanding, painting, assembly

Good fits for this service

A part that broke and isn't sold anywhere anymore. A prototype you need in hand before committing to injection molding. A batch of 50 identical brackets. A custom enclosure for electronics you're building.

Send us a sketch, a reference part, or an existing STL and we'll tell you honestly what it'll take.

How we work

We keep an eye on where supply, demand, and pricing don't line up, and act on it — sourcing goods, reselling, and moving inventory where it's needed. It's opportunistic by nature, so the specifics shift.

If you're looking for a sourcing or resale partner, reach out and describe what you need — we'll tell you quickly if it's something we can move on.

02

General Commerce

Buying, selling, and sourcing goods

Alongside our technical work, we operate a general commerce line — buying and selling across a range of product categories, sourcing inventory, and connecting supply with demand. It's less about one product category and more about spotting good opportunities and executing on them reliably.

  • Sourcing & procurement
  • Resale & inventory turnover
  • Deal & opportunity evaluation
  • Logistics coordination
03

Tech & Crypto Tools

Software & tools for tech-forward workflows

This is where our software background comes in. We build practical tools and automation — often related to crypto, mining, or general dev tooling — for our own use and for others who need something similarly hands-on and no-nonsense.

  • Custom scripts & automation tools
  • Crypto & mining-adjacent tooling
  • Small software builds & internal tools
  • Dashboards & tracking for on-the-ground operations

Have something specific in mind?

If you've got a workflow that needs automating, a tool that doesn't exist yet, or a crypto/mining-adjacent problem to solve, tell us about it.

Reach out on the contact page and we'll tell you honestly whether it's something we can help with.
