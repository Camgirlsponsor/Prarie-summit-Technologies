How the math works

Poisson odds, not a countdown

Bitcoin-style proof of work has no memory. If a block “should” have been found by now, you are not due — you just keep the same chance on the next hash.

Expected hashes per block is difficulty × 2³². Divide by your hashrate for expected seconds to a solo block. Chance of at least one block in window W is:

P = 1 − e−W / t

That is the probability of one or more blocks, not a payout estimate. Fees, stale blocks, and pool luck are ignored.

Prototype caveats

  • Live difficulty is pulled from public explorers when the browser allows it; otherwise a recent snapshot is used
  • BTCB2 is the BIP-110 / BLAKE2b Bitcoin fork — not Bitcoin II (BC2)
  • BCH2 is Bitcoin Cash II (SHA-256), a separate chain from BCH
  • This does not price coins or estimate USD revenue
